State agencies and Medicaid program integrity leaders have always operated under pressure to safeguard public resources while ensuring members receive the care they need. Today, those expectations are expanding. Recent legislative and regulatory activity across the country reflects a broader push to reduce improper payments, combat fraud, waste, and abuse (FWA), and demonstrate measurable results.
